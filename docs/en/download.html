First installation — no ML yet

  1. Verify Canon firmware 1.0.2, select M, fully charge an original Canon battery and remove accessories.
  2. Back up files before formatting. Use an SD/SDHC card of 32 GB or less for first installation, as required upstream.
  3. Format in the camera after backing up. Turn off, wait for card activity to stop and wait five seconds after opening the card door.
  4. Extract FIRST-INSTALL.zip at the card root with autoexec.bin, ML/ and ML-SETUP.FIR together, without an extra folder layer.
  5. Insert the card, turn on in M and select Canon Firmware Ver. → Firmware Update to launch the ML installer.
  6. At the green confirmation / Please restart your camera. message, restart as instructed. Do not remain on the 60-second countdown screen: it leads to uninstalling.
  7. After restart, press Delete and check Setup (+) → Version: Thai-Menu v3r5. Test ordinary photos before relying on it.
  8. If there is an error, black screen or abnormal LED activity, stop and read the included upstream Troubleshooting. Do not repeatedly flash random files.

Do not use on other cameras or Canon firmware versions. ML-SETUP.FIR is not a Canon firmware updater.

Update — already using ML

Back up the existing autoexec.bin and ML/. With the camera off, use a reader to replace both at the card root from UPDATE.zip. No format or repeat Firmware Update is needed. A new card may need separate boot preparation as described upstream.
